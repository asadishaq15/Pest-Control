About Us

Connecting homeowners with trusted local pest control providers

Who We Are

Find Your Pest Plans is a free matching service that helps homeowners connect with independent pest control providers in our network. We make it easier to find professional help for common household pest problems—from termites and rodents to mosquitoes and more.

How It Works

When you contact us by phone, your call is connected to an independent provider in our network who can discuss your pest concerns, answer questions, and help schedule an inspection or service. We do not perform pest control services ourselves.

Our Commitment

We aim to make the process of finding pest control assistance simple and transparent. Providers in our network operate independently, and homeowners are responsible for verifying licenses, insurance, and service details before hiring.

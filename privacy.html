Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Find Your Pest Plans ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit findyourpestplans.org or contact us by phone or email.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name, phone number, email address, and service location
  • Details about your pest control needs
  • Communications you send to us

We may also automatically collect limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited when you use our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Connect you with independent pest control providers in our network
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our website and matching services
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of Information

When you call or request service, your information may be shared with independent providers in our network so they can contact you about pest control services. We may also share information with service vendors who help us operate our website and phone systems, or when required by law.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information as described above to facilitate connections with service providers.

5. Phone Calls

Calls to our published phone number connect to an independent provider in our network. Call recording or monitoring may occur for quality and training purposes, subject to applicable law.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand site usage and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. You may also opt out of marketing communications if you receive them.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to adults and homeowners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our services after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact us at info@findyourpestplans.org or (888) 975-9214.
